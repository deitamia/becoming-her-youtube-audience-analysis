## 8. Potential Content Opportunities

The following are initial directions to investigate, not proven
audience needs:

- Practical confidence-building and self-worth discussions.
- Self-discovery and identity during your twenties.
- Building habits and routines without unrealistic expectations.
- Financial independence and personal responsibility.
- Honest personal transformation stories.

These directions should be validated through public comment analysis,
additional competitor research, and a larger sample.

## 9. Research Limitations

- The sample contains 120 videos, not all relevant YouTube videos.
- Search results were relevance-ranked.
- Public view, like, and comment counts are not private analytics.
- Video age and format influence performance.
- Keyword-based themes can misclassify videos.
- A high view count does not establish audience satisfaction.
- The sample does not establish viewer age, gender, or location.
- Engagement ratios are descriptive and should not be interpreted
  as causal measures of content quality.
